Employee Leave Policy
Effective 1 April 2024. Supersedes the 2021 policy in full.

Clause 3.1 - Annual leave entitlement

Full-time employees accrue 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar
year, accruing monthly in arrears at 2.08 days per completed month of service.
Part-time employees accrue pro rata on the same schedule. Leave accrued but not
taken may be carried into the following year up to a maximum of 5 days, and
carried-over days expire on 31 March if unused.

Clause 3.2 - Requesting leave

Requests are submitted through the leave portal and require approval from the
direct line manager. Any absence of 5 consecutive working days or more requires
at least 14 calendar days of notice. Shorter absences require 3 working days of
notice. A manager who does not respond within 5 working days is deemed to have
approved the request.

Clause 4.2 - Sick leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of fully paid sick leave per calendar year.
Absence of more than 3 consecutive working days requires a medical certificate
submitted within 7 days of returning to work. Sick leave does not accrue and
does not carry over. Sickness occurring during booked annual leave may be
reclassified as sick leave if a certificate is provided.

Clause 5.4 - Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 18 weeks of paid parental leave and secondary
caregivers receive 6 weeks, in both cases after 12 months of continuous
service. Parental leave may be taken in up to three separate blocks within the
24 months following birth or placement. Notice of 8 weeks is required before the
intended start of the first block.

Clause 6.1 - Unpaid leave and sabbatical

After four years of continuous service an employee may apply for an unpaid
sabbatical of between one and three months. Approval is discretionary and rests
with the department head, who must consider team coverage. Benefits continue
during a sabbatical but annual leave does not accrue.

Clause 7.3 - Escalation of a refused request

An employee whose leave request is refused may escalate in writing to the head
of department within 10 working days of the refusal. The head of department
responds within 10 working days, and that response is final. Escalations are
recorded and reviewed by People Operations each quarter.

Clause 8.1 - Leave on termination

Accrued and untaken annual leave is paid out at the final salary rate. Leave
taken in excess of accrual is deducted from the final payment. Carried-over
days from the previous year are included in the payout calculation.
